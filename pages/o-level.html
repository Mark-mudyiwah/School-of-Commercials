O-LEVEL PROGRAMME

Our O-Level Subjects

Build a strong academic foundation through practical learning, essential knowledge and examination preparation.

Business Studies

Understand how businesses operate, make decisions, manage resources and respond to changing markets.

What You'll Cover

  • Business ownership and entrepreneurship
  • Business objectives and management
  • Marketing and market research
  • Production and business operations
  • Business finance and communication

Accounting

Develop financial skills by learning how to record, organise and interpret business transactions accurately.

What You'll Cover

  • Double-entry bookkeeping
  • Journals and ledger accounts
  • Trial balances and corrections
  • Financial statements
  • Bank reconciliation and depreciation

Mathematics

Strengthen logical thinking and problem-solving skills through mathematical concepts and real-life applications.

What You'll Cover

  • Numbers, fractions and percentages
  • Algebra and equations
  • Geometry and mensuration
  • Graphs, functions and statistics
  • Probability, ratios and trigonometry

Commerce

Explore how goods and services move from producers to consumers and how trade supports the economy.

What You'll Cover

  • Trade and distribution
  • Retailing and wholesaling
  • Banking and insurance
  • Transport and communication
  • Advertising and business documents
